## 4. Module map for the port

Ownership rule: React renders all DOM once. Everything the loop touches per frame lives in a plain
mutable module (`world/store.ts`), never in React state. React state is used only for UI that changes
on a click (claim list, trace panel, cause buttons, summary lines), which architecture rule 3 allows
because it never re-renders per frame.

## 5. Behaviour checklist

Numbered across phases. Each item is checked against the reference at its phase's stop.

### V1. World core and overview

1. 41 documents (24 tender pages, 12 evidence files, 5 drafts) rendered once as real HTML with the reference's exact copy.
2. Three nested layers per document: `.cw` (translate, rotate, fine scale, `will-change: transform`), `.sc` (coarse scale), `.card`; glow layers `.ga` and `.gr` inside `.sc`.
3. Templates: tender page (six bars on mandatory pages, eight on others; `clause` on mandatory), CV with the cited row marked `.ct`, ISO certificate, CA turnover certificate, completion certificate, bank guarantee, rate schedule, registration, draft section, readiness gate.
4. Projection `s = F / (F - min(z, 0.55F))`, on-screen scale `min(1, s * G)`, position `W.c + x * scale`.
5. Per-arrangement fit `Gs[k]` in `measure()`: projected bounds of documents with opacity at least 0.5; width factor 0.94 portrait, 0.72 helix, 0.86 otherwise; height factor 0.84, unbounded for the helix.
6. Scroll holds: a pinned (sticky) section holds for its whole pin; others hold around their centre by `vh * data-hold` (default 0.12); the first hold starts at minus infinity, the last runs to infinity.
7. `pOf()` maps smoothed scroll (`exp(-7 dt)`, raw under reduced motion) to chapter progress.
8. Morph between neighbouring arrangements with `ease`, per-document stagger up to 0.22.
9. Coarse/fine scale split with `QSTEP = 24`; change-only writes of transform, opacity, z-index and visibility; documents under opacity 0.004 are hidden.
10. Depth fade into the dark (0.72 dark theme, 0.4 light).
11. `#atmo` follows the world centre by transform, written only when it moves more than 0.5 px.
12. One rAF loop, dt clamped at 0.05 s, idle while the tab is hidden.
13. World rect: on desktop beside the text column (gutter, column width and 28 px), below a 60 px header; in portrait, the top 56% of the viewport.
14. `measure()` on load, resize, body `ResizeObserver` and `fonts.ready`.
15. `pile`: relaxed sphere (golden-angle seed, 70 relaxation passes), shuffled slots, slow auto-spin, scroll-driven yaw, mouse parallax, pitch clamp, per-page tilt up to 7 degrees.
16. `park`: documents not in play sit in a slow, far, dim shell (opacity 0.16); drafts hidden.
17. Mouse parallax from mouse pointers only, eased, frozen while dragging and under reduced motion.
18. Drag to rotate on the overview: starts after 6 px, yaw and pitch per pixel as in the reference, inertia decays at `exp(-2.4 dt)`, pitch relaxes back, holding still 90 ms before release cancels inertia.
19. Touch: a sideways swipe rotates, anything else scrolls; `touch-action: pan-y` on the hero under `hover: none`.
20. Cursors: `grab` over the world on the overview, `grabbing` during a drag; the text column, header, footer, links and buttons never start a drag.
21. Header: mark, `#where` chapter number and name (updated only when the nearest chapter changes, `aria-live`), nav links.
22. All six chapter sections in place with their copy, ids, `data-ch`, `data-hold` and layout classes (needed by `measure()` for holds before their interaction is built).
23. Hero copy, buttons, and the note that switches between mouse and touch wording.
24. Grain in `body::before`, footer.

### V2. Tender analysis and eligibility check

25. `pack` helix: 24 pages in two seamless turns, seen slightly from above, travelling with scroll, mouse parallax, ends faded.
26. Mandatory pages stay at least 0.82 opacity on the far side; all six lit (amber ring, glow, clause border).
27. Labels beside the six mandatory pages, on the outer side relative to the world centre.
28. Index list "p. N, full name" built from the mandatory pages.
29. Hover or focus on a row lights its page (`.hot`, 10 px ring) and its label (`.on`).
30. Hover on a mandatory page lights its row; pointer cursor.
31. Clicking a row or a mandatory page spins it to the front via `bringToFront()` (nearest wrap, eased at `exp(-3.5 dt)`).
32. Without hover, the row of the page squarely facing the reader (facing above 0.86) is marked.
33. `match` arrangement: requirement pages in a top row, evidence in a bottom row; portrait grid.
34. States: met lit amber, partially met slate, not met alarm with red glow.
35. Requirement labels above (page, short name, coloured status), width tied to scale.
36. Evidence labels below (short names).
37. Wires from each requirement to its evidence: solid amber for met, dashed slate for partial; a short dashed alarm stub below the subcontracting page.
38. Labels and wires fade with chapter weight; no labels in portrait.
39. Tally copy.
40. Side alternation per `SIDE`: the world slides between the left and right rects, two scrims cross-fade, columns on `.r` chapters align right. Portrait does not alternate and has no scrims.

### V3. Evidence tracing

41. `proof` arrangement: evidence wall in two staggered rows curving away at the sides; portrait grid.
42. Claims in the draft sheet; selecting one marks it `.on` and `aria-pressed`, and keeps its verdict mark (tick, cross, dash).
43. Trace panel per verdict (three hops when supported; the reason when unsupported or a commitment); counter and the final summary sentence.
44. Reset clears selection, verdicts, trace and counter.
45. The cited document moves to the reading plane beside the text at natural reading size (scale up to 1), upright, on top.
46. Other evidence dims while a claim is selected.
47. The cited line is highlighted on the document (`.ct` under `lit`).
48. Thread from the live position of the selected sentence to the cited line, using `CT` offsets, drawn in at 1.5 per second, with a glow for supported claims; colour per verdict.
49. Dead-end thread for unsupported and flagged claims, with an end ring once fully drawn.
50. The sentence position is read with `getBoundingClientRect` once at frame start, before any writes.
51. Phone reader: in portrait, the cited document is shown inline below the sheet at full column width; refreshed on resize; no thread in portrait.

### V4. Change impact

52. `impact` arrangement: four columns (sources, requirements, sections, gate); in portrait the columns become rows.
53. Node labels below each document.
54. Graph edges as curves (horizontal on desktop, vertical in portrait).
55. Cause buttons with `aria-pressed`; `seed()` computes hops breadth first over `EDGES`.
56. The wave advances at 0.9 hops per second.
57. Each edge on the wave draws along its length with a pulse at its head, and retracts smoothly when the cause changes.
58. Documents turn `src` or `hit` as the wave reaches them and swell 7% over 0.6 s; their labels turn alarm-coloured.
59. The readiness gate stamp switches to Blocked when the wave reaches it.
60. Summary lines appear in step with the wave; correct singular and plural forms; total of affected items; idle prompt before any selection.

### V5. Submission and direct manipulation

61. `submission` stack of 17 pages, executive summary on top, slight offsets and tilts.
62. Clicking a page on the overview flings it outward; it shrinks, fades and refiles into its slot after 1.6 s.
63. Holding a page on the overview (220 ms) lifts it; it follows the pointer and tilts with its speed; it is thrown above 380 px/s, otherwise it settles back.
64. Stack: drag the top page and throw it, or click to toss it sideways (alternating sides); it moves to the bottom of the stack and slides back in from the side it left.
65. Flight physics: gravity, air drag, spin, exit bounds and a 2.8 s limit.
66. Thrown pages move into `#flying` above the content and back into `#cards` on return; `.held` shadow while held.
67. Touch: a tap throws, a sideways swipe rotates.
68. Grab cursor over the stack.

### V6. Hardening

69. Light theme toggle (`data-theme`, button label, depth fade 0.4).
70. 390 px layout: world in the top 56% of the viewport, text below with its fade, pinned chapters unpinned, `#where` and nav links hidden.
71. Short desktop viewports (height up to 780 px) compact the type and spacing.
72. Reduced motion: no auto-spin or parallax, raw scroll, morphs snap at the halfway point, eased values snap, thread and wave complete at once, no swell, no summary animation, transitions near zero.
73. Keyboard: visible focus ring, focusing an index row acts as hover, every control is a real button or link.
74. Accessibility: world layers `aria-hidden`; live regions on `#where`, the trace panel and the impact summary.
75. Safe-area insets respected.
76. Perf check on every chapter; dead code and unused dependencies removed.
